Account & data

You can permanently delete your ClassCheck account and its Supabase data. This is irreversible. Other teachers cannot delete your account.

Permanently delete account?

Warning: This permanently removes your account and stored ClassCheck data. It cannot be undone.

  • Your account, profile, attendance, and saved sessions will be deleted.
  • If you are an adviser, your owned classes, student rosters, and attendance from all teachers in those classes will also be deleted.
  • If you are a subject teacher, your class memberships and your attendance will be deleted.
